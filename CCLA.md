# tuff Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

This agreement is for a company or other legal entity (the "Corporation")
whose employees or contractors contribute to tuff (the "Project"), owned by
Brenden Forward (the "Owner"). It is modelled on the Apache Software
Foundation Corporate CLA and uses the same terms as the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md), with these changes:

1. "You" means the Corporation. Contributions are those submitted by the
   employees and contractors the Corporation designates in Schedule A
   ("Designated Contributors"), which the Corporation may update by written
   notice to the Owner.
2. The Corporation grants the copyright license (section 2), patent license
   (section 3) and relicensing rights (section 4) of the Individual CLA for
   all Contributions of its Designated Contributors.
3. The Corporation represents that each Designated Contributor is authorized
   to submit Contributions on its behalf, and that it is legally entitled to
   grant these licenses.
4. The Corporation will notify the Owner when a person is no longer a
   Designated Contributor. Contributions made before that notice remain
   covered.
5. Each Designated Contributor still signs the Individual CLA.

To sign, send a signed copy of this document with Schedule A to the Owner
(contact details in the repository's README or GitHub profile).
